## Full description (4000 char max)

Get moving in Abuja with HiGO — book a keke or car ride in seconds, track your driver live, and pay however you like.

WHY RIDE WITH HIGO
• Book a ride in a few taps — pickup and destination, done
• See your fare upfront before you book
• Track your driver's live location and ETA
• Pay by cash or card, securely processed by Paystack
• Rate every trip and build a driver you trust
• In-app chat and calling with your driver
• 24/7 support if anything goes wrong

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign up with your phone number
2. Set your pickup and destination
3. Get matched with a nearby verified driver
4. Track your ride and pay when you arrive

SAFETY FIRST
Every HiGO driver is identity- and vehicle-verified before they can accept trips. You can see your driver's name, photo, and vehicle details before the ride starts, and share your trip status with anyone you choose.

Currently serving the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), Abuja.

Questions or support: support@hiconnectgo.com

## Data Safety form — quick reference

| Data type | Collected? | Purpose | Shared? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Precise location | Yes | App functionality (ride booking, live tracking) | Shared with matched driver during a trip |
| Name | Yes | Account management, app functionality | Shared with matched driver |
| Phone number | Yes | Account management (OTP verification) | Not shared publicly |
| Financial info (payment method) | Yes | App functionality (fare payment) | Processed via Paystack |
| App activity / device IDs | Yes | Analytics, crash reporting, push notifications | Not shared |

- **Background location:** Not required for the passenger app (location is only used in-app while booking/tracking a ride) — should NOT need the sensitive background-location approval process the driver app needs.
- **Data deletion:** Available on request via support@hiconnectgo.com.
