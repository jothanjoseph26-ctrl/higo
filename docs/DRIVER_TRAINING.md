## 1. Welcome to HiGO

HiGO is a ride-hailing platform serving **Abuja (FCT)** and **Delta State** (Warri, Asaba, and surrounding towns). As a HiGO driver, you are an independent partner who earns money by completing passenger trips and parcel deliveries.

**Key facts:**
- You keep **90%** of every fare (10% platform commission)
- You choose your own hours — go online whenever you want
- Cash and digital payments both supported
- SOS emergency button always available
- Free 14-day trial period after registration (no subscription required)

## 2. Getting Started

### Registration (Onboarding)

When you first open the driver app, you will see a 5-step onboarding wizard:

| Step | What You Provide |
|------|-----------------|
| **Step 1 — Welcome** | Introduction to HiGO |
| **Step 2 — Personal Info** | Full name, phone number, email, NIN (National Identification Number) |
| **Step 3 — Vehicle Info** | Vehicle type (Keke/Car), plate number, model, color, engine & chassis numbers |
| **Step 4 — Documents** | Upload identity, license, vehicle registration, insurance, roadworthiness |

**After submission:**

- You can track status in the app under **Verification Status**
- Once approved, you can go online and start earning

**Important:** You get a **14-day grace period** after signup — no subscription needed to go online during this period.

## 3. Going Online & Offline

### Going Online

1. Open the driver app — you will see the **Home** screen with a map
2. Tap the large green **"GO ONLINE"** button
3. The system checks:
   - ✅ Your KYC documents are approved
   - ✅ Your subscription is active (or you are within 14-day grace period)
4. If checks pass, you are now online and will see: **"Waiting for trip requests..."**
5. Your GPS location is now being shared with HiGO so passengers can find you

### Going Offline

1. Tap **"GO OFFLINE"** on the Home screen
2. You will stop receiving ride requests
3. Any pending ride request will be dismissed

**Tip:** Stay online during peak hours (morning rush 7–10am, evening rush 4–7pm) to maximize earnings.

### GPS Requirements

- Your phone's GPS must be ON with **High Accuracy** enabled
- Stay within Nigeria (latitude 4–14, longitude 3–15)
- If GPS is off, you cannot go online

## 4. Receiving & Accepting Ride Requests

### How Matching Works

When a passenger requests a ride:
1. HiGO finds the nearest online drivers in the same city
2. Drivers are ranked by a **Trust Score** based on:
   - Your rating (higher = better)
   - Number of completed trips (more = better)
   - Verification level (full KYC = better)
   - Distance to pickup (closer = better)
3. Up to **2 drivers** receive the offer at the same time
4. The first driver to accept gets the trip

### When You Receive a Request

1. You will hear a **ringtone** and feel **vibration** (even if your screen is off)
2. A trip request card appears showing:
   - 📍 **Pickup address** — where the passenger is
   - 🏁 **Destination** — where they want to go
   - 💰 **Payout** — how much you will earn
   - 📏 **Distance** — how far the pickup is from you
   - ⏱️ **15-second countdown** — you have 15 seconds to decide

### Accept or Decline

- **Accept** → Tap **"Accept"** — navigation to the passenger begins
- **Decline** → Tap **"Decline"** — the offer goes to the next driver
- **Timeout** → If you do nothing for 15 seconds, the offer is auto-declined and goes to another driver

**Tip:** Accept quickly! The first driver to accept gets the trip.

## 5. Complete Trip Lifecycle

### Phase 1: Navigate to Pickup

After accepting:
- The app shows a **map** with the route to the passenger
- You see the passenger's **name** and can **call** or **chat** with them
- Drive to the pickup location following the GPS route

### Phase 2: Arrive at Pickup

- When you reach the passenger, tap **"Confirm Arrival"**
- ⚠️ **You must be within 500 meters of the pickup point** — the GPS will verify this
- If you are too far, you will get an error: *"You are X km from the pickup location. Please move closer."*

### Phase 3: Start the Trip

- After the passenger gets in, tap **"Start Trip"**
- ⚠️ **You must still be within 500 meters of pickup** to start
- The route switches to the destination
- The passenger can now see your live location on their app

### Phase 4: Complete the Trip

- When you arrive at the destination, tap **"Complete Trip"**
- The fare summary appears:
  - Total fare
  - Distance and duration
  - Payment method (Cash or Card)
- **Rate the passenger** with 1–5 stars
- Tap **"Back to Home"** to return to the main screen

### Payment Collection

| Payment Type | What Happens |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Cash** | Passenger pays you directly. Collect the exact amount shown. |
| **Card/Transfer** | Payment processed digitally. Your share (90%) is added to your earnings. |

## 6. Payments & Earnings

### How Earnings Work

- **Gross Fare** = Base fare + Distance fare + Surge bonus (if any)
- **Platform Fee** = 10% of gross fare
- **Your Earnings** = Gross Fare − Platform Fee (you keep 90%)

### Example

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Base Fare | ₦500 |
| Distance Fare (5 km) | ₦1,000 |
| **Gross Fare** | **₦1,500** |
| Platform Fee (10%) | −₦150 |
| **Your Earnings** | **₦1,350** |

### Viewing Your Earnings

1. Go to **Earnings** from the menu
2. Toggle between **Daily / Weekly / Monthly** views
3. See your earnings chart and breakdown
4. View individual trip earnings with full fare details

### Withdrawing Money

- Tap **"Withdraw to Bank"** on the Earnings screen
- Enter your bank details
- Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours

## 8. Navigation & GPS

### How Navigation Works

- After accepting a trip, the app shows a **live map** with the route
- Turn-by-turn instructions appear at the top of the screen
- ETA (estimated time of arrival) and distance are shown in real-time
- GPS updates are sent to the passenger so they can track you

### Route Recalculation

- The route updates automatically if you go off-route
- If you lose GPS signal, the app will try to reconnect

### Voice Navigation

- When **Voice Mode** is enabled, the app speaks instructions aloud
- Supports English, Hausa, Pidgin, and Yoruba
- Useful for keeping your eyes on the road

### GPS Validation Rules

| Action | Distance Requirement |
|--------|---------------------|
| Confirm Arrival | Within 500m of pickup |
| Start Trip | Within 500m of pickup |

## 10. Fare Negotiation

Passengers can propose a lower fare than the estimated price. When this happens:

1. You receive a **negotiation request** on your Home screen
2. You have three options:
   - **Accept** — Take the passenger's offered fare
   - **Counter (Recommended)** — Suggest a fair middle price
   - **Counter (Custom)** — Enter your own price
3. The negotiation goes back and forth until both agree or it expires

**Tip:** Be flexible but fair. A slightly lower fare is better than no fare at all.

## 12. Safety & SOS Emergency

### Safety Rules (Must Follow)

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| 1 | **Verify Your Passenger** — Ask "Who are you waiting for?" NOT "Are you [name]?" |
| 2 | **Stay on Approved Routes** — Follow the GPS, avoid unlit or restricted areas |
| 3 | **Keep Phone Charged** — Start your shift with 50%+ battery, carry a power bank |
| 4 | **Maximum Passengers** — Keke: 3 passengers max. Car: 4 passengers max. NEVER exceed. |
| 5 | **Trust Your Instincts** — If something feels wrong, cancel the trip or drive to a police station |
| 6 | **SOS Is Always Available** — Tap the SOS button to alert the HiGO Safety Team instantly |

### Pre-Shift Checklist

- [ ] Check brakes and tires
- [ ] Ensure horn and lights work
- [ ] Wear seatbelt (car drivers)
- [ ] Phone charged to 50%+
- [ ] Carry power bank
- [ ] Take a 15-minute break every 3 hours

### SOS Emergency Button

If you feel unsafe at any time:

1. Tap the **SOS** button (available on the Home screen and during trips)
2. Confirm the emergency
3. **What happens:**
   - Your **live GPS location** is shared immediately
   - All your **emergency contacts** receive an SMS with your location
   - The **HiGO Safety Team** is alerted with your details
   - Trip recording continues for evidence
4. You can **cancel SOS** if the danger has passed

**Tip:** Add your emergency contacts in the app before you start driving.

### What to Do in an Emergency

1. **Stay calm** and drive to the nearest police station if possible
2. **Tap SOS** to alert HiGO Safety Team
3. **Call 112** (Nigeria Emergency) or **199** (Police)
4. **Do not argue** with aggressive passengers — pull over safely and end the trip

## 14. Ratings & Performance

### How Ratings Work

- After every trip, the passenger rates you 1–5 stars
- You also rate the passenger (helps HiGO keep the platform safe)

### Why Ratings Matter

| Rating | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| **4.8+** | Priority matching — you get trip offers first |
| **4.0–4.7** | Normal matching |
| **Below 4.0** | Reduced trip requests — fewer opportunities |
| **Below 3.0** | Account review — may be suspended |

### Tips for High Ratings

1. **Be polite** — Greet passengers, be friendly
2. **Drive smoothly** — Avoid sudden braking or speeding
3. **Follow the GPS** — Don't take unauthorized shortcuts
4. **Keep your vehicle clean** — Interior and exterior
5. **Be on time** — Arrive at pickup promptly
6. **Verify the passenger** — Ask "Who are you waiting for?"

## 16. Troubleshooting & FAQ

### Common Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Can't go online** | Check if KYC is approved. Check if subscription is active (or within 14-day trial). |
| **No trip requests** | Make sure GPS is ON with High Accuracy. Check internet connection. Try going offline and back online. |
| **App not ringing** | Check phone volume. Check notification permissions. Restart the app. |
| **GPS not accurate** | Move to an open area. Restart phone. Check location settings. |
| **Can't confirm arrival** | You must be within 500m of the pickup point. Move closer and try again. |
| **Payment not showing** | Wait 24 hours. Check Earnings page. Contact support if still missing. |
| **App crashed** | Restart the app. Your trip progress is saved on the server. |
| **Lost internet mid-trip** | Keep driving — GPS and trip data are saved. The app will reconnect automatically. |

### Support Contact

- **Phone:** 080 3397 5037
- **In-App:** Go to Menu → Support → Live Chat
- **Available:** 24/7 for emergencies, 8am–8pm for general support

### Important Reminders

1. **Always verify your passenger** before starting the trip
2. **Never exceed passenger capacity** (3 for keke, 4 for car)
3. **Keep your phone charged** during shifts
4. **Take breaks** every 3 hours
5. **Use SOS** if you feel unsafe
6. **Rate passengers** after every trip
7. **Keep documents updated** — expired documents will block you from going online
